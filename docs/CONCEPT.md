# RARE EARTH — concept (v3, "keep listening")

Written before looking at the other two versions or the techno-remix film. Only the shared band assets on
`main` (character sheets, 2011 photos, robot mascot) were looked at first. Revised once, per the brief:
**Earth never transmits.** Many viewers carry a *Three-Body Problem* "dark forest" fear, so nobody should leave
thinking we beamed our location at another planet. In this film we only listen; the other world is the one that
chose to shine.

> **Logline.** In 2011 she wrote a song for the people who listen to the sky. In 2026 a star started blinking in time.

## 1. What the lyrics are actually doing

The song is a **duet sung by one voice**, and the pronouns keep swapping sides.

| Lines | Who speaks | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| *Do you still care / You're yearning to see the life out there / Searching for me / Are you still there / A rare earth looking for a friend* | **The other world**, speaking to us | "You" (Earth) are yearning and searching; "me" is the life out there. They are a rare earth too, and they have been shining a beacon, asking whether anyone still listens. |
| *Lived my life on a pale blue dot / Your signal here I think I've caught / The beating blinking of a star / A planet's transit is not that far from my own / How could we be alone* | **Earth**, speaking to them | We catch *their* signal: a star whose light blinks in a rhythm no nature makes. It is a transit beacon, with structures in orbit dimming their sun on purpose (a real SETI proposal). The planet behind it has an orbit "not that far from my own". |
| *Before they launch or self-destruct / Weapons, wars, and now we're ██████ / Keep on looking, keep the faith / Keep up funding, our planet waits / for your transmission / Our science has a vision* | **Earth, confessing** | The Great Filter: civilisations either launch or self-destruct. The censored word is a literal audio drop-out (64.45–65.40 s). Then the turn to optimism is a plea to keep *listening*: "our planet waits for **your** transmission". In April 2011 the Allen Telescope Array went into hibernation for lack of funding, the same year this song was written for a SETI event. |
| V1 again | **Mirror** | The other world, zoomed out: they have kept their star blinking for a thousand years without knowing whether anyone is there. "Are you still there?" is also our question to them, because their light is centuries old. |
| V2 again | **Confirmation** | The pattern repeats, and more instruments confirm it. "How could we be alone" stops being a question. |

Both planets are rare earths. The film holds the asymmetry calmly: **we listen, they shine.** Earth's side is
dishes, screens, patience and funding. Theirs is a lighthouse.

## 2. The idea on screen: *listening*

* **Energy always flows *into* our instruments.** Dish focus points glow when light arrives. Incoming
  wavefronts, never outgoing beams. No radio bubble around Earth, and no Golden Record, Pioneer or Arecibo
  "here is where we live" imagery.
* **The signal is a rhythm.** The beacon star dims and brightens on the song's beat. The universe keeps
  time with the song, which is the music video's one piece of magic.
* **People are drawn in light.** Every character shot is rotoscoped. Seedance 2.5 plates give the performance
  and physics, and a JavaScript renderer redraws each frame as ink, flat tone and rim light. The plates never
  appear on screen.
* **Earth is pale blue, the other world is ember, the signal is gold.** Red only appears in the Great Filter.
* **Easter egg:** the beacon star is catalogued **LGM-2**, the successor to LGM-1 ("Little Green Men"), the
  1967 pulsar that was briefly mistaken for aliens.

## 4. Attention design (K-pop / anime-OP grammar)

* **Frame 0 is a poster.** A huge lyric over a strong image, readable as a thumbnail.
* **Lyrics carry the first 17 seconds.** Poster-scale type that fills the frame, each word hitting on its sung onset.
  After that the scale varies: hero cards on hooks, subtitle-scale on verses, type embedded in data UI on
  technical lines.
* **Composition reserves space for type.** Plates are framed with the character on the thirds and negative
  space where the lyric lands (character right, lyric left, and the reverse in the mirror act).
* **One signature gesture per chorus** (a K-pop "point move"): a cupped hand to the ear, *listening*, on
  *are you still there*. It repeats and mirrors in V4.
* **Anime-OP beats:** title slam, sky pans, character name cards, an emotional close-up with glinting eyes, a
  final group shot.
* **Edit energy tracks the song:** smash cuts on downbeats; impact frames (1–2 inverted line-art frames) on the
  biggest hits; speed ramps into the launch and the climax; long takes in the breakdown.
* **Twitter-legible references:** the Pale Blue Dot, the Wow! printout, Kepler light curves, a VLA/ATA dish
  array (*Contact*), the SETI@home screensaver, the booster catch, Artemis II "Earthset", the far-side lunar
  radio telescope, the redaction bar, "it's so over / we're so back", Macross (a singer's song meets another
  civilisation).

## 5. Look (to be locked in the style bible)

* **Night first, light is the subject.** Most of the film sits at blue hour or night; it ends in dawn gold and
  white-hot confirmation.
* **Characters:** ink contour (variable width, tapered), 2–3 flat tones from a per-scene ramp, and a hard rim
  light. Drawn on twos (12 drawings per second), like TV anime. Camera, FX and type run on ones.
* **Environments:** procedural JS (stars, skies, dishes, planets, data) wherever possible, so layout and beat
  timing are exact. Seedance supplies environments only where physics sells the shot: plume, ocean, crowds,
  cloth, hair.
* **Palette:** night `#060a1a`, pale blue `#8ecbff`, paper white `#f4f1e8`, signal gold `#ffcf5a`, ember
  `#ff8a4c`, violet `#7b61ff`, filter red `#ff2d3d` (bridge only).
* **Type:** a condensed heavy grotesk for hero lyrics, a monospace for telemetry, and a heavy Mincho serif for
  anime-style title cards (with small katakana/hangul accents).

## 6. What the earlier versions already did (so this one doesn't)

The earlier versions were watched after this document's first draft. They already cover: a cozy
rediscovery-in-the-bedroom story with ghostly bandmates (blissful-bohr); split-screen 2011/2026 anime, an
*Unknown Pleasures* pulsar plot for "blinking", a donation page for "keep up funding", JWST for "science has
a vision", red bars for "wars", and an "are you still there? — yes." ending (affectionate-ride); and blue
risograph anime with neon edge-traced characters and a ringed pink exoplanet (techno remix). None of those
devices are reused here.
