# RARE EARTH — "keep listening" (M/V, 2026)

A music video for **"Rare Earth"** by RNA (Robot Ninja Apocalypse). Jade wrote the song in 2011 for a SETI
event, and the track here has her vocals re-added.

> In 2011 she wrote a song for the people who listen to the sky. In 2026 a star started blinking in time.

* **Video:** `video/rare_earth_keep_listening.mp4` (1080p24, the original song, unmodified)
* **Concept and lyric reading:** [`docs/CONCEPT.md`](docs/CONCEPT.md)
* **Style bible:** [`docs/STYLE.md`](docs/STYLE.md)
* **How it was made:** [`docs/PIPELINE.md`](docs/PIPELINE.md)

## The idea

The song is a duet sung by one voice.

* **Verse 1** is the other world asking whether anyone is still listening.
* **Verse 2** is Earth catching their signal: a star whose light blinks in a rhythm no nature makes, a *transit
  beacon*. In the film, that rhythm is the song's own kick drum.
* **The bridge** is the Great Filter. It ends in a plea to *keep listening*: in April 2011, the year the song was
  written, the Allen Telescope Array went into hibernation for lack of funding.

Earth only ever listens in this film. Energy flows into our dishes, never out of them. The other world is seen
only from far away.

## Look

A 1988 space-opera OVA (Macross, Gunbuster, *Wings of Honnêamise*) re-inked by a JavaScript renderer, with a
2020s editorial type system on top. Seedance 2.5 plates give the performances, and every frame is traced and
redrawn in the browser, so the generated video never appears on screen.
